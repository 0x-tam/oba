## 1. Lebanese Concept House / Twig kit

### Identity that must survive simplification

This is not generic scaffolding or a set of wooden tables. The distinctive construction consists of: tall pale square timber stems rising from cylindrical concrete ballast bases; pairs of parallel timber branches clamping either side of a stem; bolts visibly crossing the connection; branches overlapping in perpendicular directions at different levels; gray-painted rail tips; removable white display platforms in circle, square and triangle shapes. The independent platforms rest on the branches and have a small locating block underneath. Store photograph shows freestanding islands as well as taller hanging-display frames and multiple shelf heights.

### Geometry recipe

Suggested display cell (illustrative scale, not certified): stem square 0.06×0.06, height 1.45. Stem base is a warm-gray concrete cylinder radius 0.13, height 0.40; stem penetrates centrally into its top. Keep stem axis square and crisp. Base is much broader than the stem—about 4× stem width diameter—and about 5–7× stem width tall. Photo pp9 lower right is the best base detail.

Between two stems spaced ~0.85, make TWO rectangular rails on either side of their centerline, separated by a slot slightly wider than the stem. Each rail section about 0.025 thick ×0.05 tall. At a stem: rails occupy z±0.0475, run along X, with total length = stem spacing+0.22 (roughly0.11 projection past each stem). Bottom of branch below stem tip by ~0.10. Add second perpendicular branch pair below first by one rail depth; this gives the visible interlocking layered node. A branch is not a single centered beam.

Silver fastener head: short metallic cylinder/disc ~0.014 diameter oriented across rail thickness through node; washer slightly larger, on each visible outer face. Bolt centers align with stem axis. Gray paint caps the small rectangular end faces of rails and visible top face of the stem (photo pp8). Add thin cap meshes, not a gray covering of whole beam.

At a rail midpoint, removable top options: circle diameter0.55, square0.53×0.53, right/equilateral-ish triangle width0.60 (image does not certify exact triangle angles). White matte plate thickness≈0.022–0.03. Platform underside has a solid wooden rectangular locating block that fits into the slot BETWEEN paired rails; photo pp9 upper right clearly shows it as the plate is lifted. Block about0.15–0.22 long ×0.04–0.05 wide ×0.07 deep. Plate sits atop rails with block dropped in the gap. This is a meaningful model detail and explains modularity.

Assemble a zigzag or U-shaped chain of 5–7 stems in plan, not a uniform cube grid. Use branch connections at e.g.0.8,1.10,1.35; additional stems can rise to2.0 for a hanging rail. Add one low white pedestal cube nearby only as photographic context; it is not integral to the kit. If displaying products, simple opaque bottles/books suffice; avoid obscuring joints.

### Interactions / annotations

Part annotations grounded in text/photos:
- “Stem — square timber upright”
- “Weighted foot — freestanding concrete cylinder”
- “Paired branches — bolted rail connection”
- “Locating block — platform slots between rails”
- “Display surface — interchangeable circle, square or triangle”
- “Layout — a kit that adapts to merchandise and floor plan”

Useful controls: assembled/exploded joint; swap platform shape; linear/zigzag/island arrangement; show one node close-up. Explode platform upward0.35, locating block attached; slide paired rails outward±0.15; raise stem from base0.4. Do not animate stem bending or rails revolving while supposedly still bolted. Physically plausible reconfiguration can fade between layouts or separate/reassemble components.

## 2. Artist’s Studio Balcony / Afaf Zurayk

### Identity that must survive simplification

The project is an adaptive white/light-gray thin metal grid that becomes both storage wall and balcony enclosure. It starts from a20×20 module (the source does not explicitly print a unit, convention suggests cm; safest label “20×20 base module”). It is selectively carved into40,60 and100 square openings and elongated voids for paintings, easels, books, planters and drying racks. Actual photograph p13 left also shows closely spaced curved steel ribs: vertical uprights bend smoothly through a quarter-circle into horizontal overhead beams. A simple wireframe box alone misses this curved built detail.

### Recommended scene composition

An L-shaped material study: a long storage wall on the rear/left plus adjacent open balcony canopy on the right. Cut away opaque apartment walls so model remains readable. Source p12 physical model shows storage wall left-rear and dense roof lattice right; p13 built photo shows the curved transition; they need not be treated as dimensionally identical construction drawings.

Illustrative envelope: storage wall length4.0, height2.6, depth0.4; balcony canopy approx3.2wide×3.6deep at2.7height. Use module m=0.20 and square metal section0.018–0.025. Structural edge posts might be0.035. Color off-white with slight warm-gray shadowing. Floor light mottled terrazzo/concrete; parapet pale opaque wall around0.55–0.7high. Geometry must be true slender beams with open gaps, not opaque wall + grid texture.

Storage wall: start conceptual20×13 grid at m. Then CARVE larger openings by removing internal subdivisions, keeping surrounding perimeter members. Use a list of cell rectangles with no overlaps, e.g. large canvas openings5×5 cells; smaller3×3,2×2 and1×1. Add some vertical2×4 or3×5 openings. Not every cell needs an independent full cube; merge shared edges to avoid double thickness. Render front and rear grids separated0.35, connected at surviving corners by depth bars; add select thin warm-white/light-wood shelf planes. Example distribution for visual interest: xcells0–4/ycells0–5: large painting opening; x5–7/y0–3: upright canvas slot group; x8–12/y0–4: large canvas opening; top two rows mostly small cells; x13–19 mixed tall and small openings. This is a generic arrangement inspired by the source, not an exact tracing of every grid cell.

For a closer trace, p13 upper-right diagram is a roughly20-column×13-row layout (count approximate) with conspicuous square voids. Pixel-detected or manually traced larger openings can replace procedural cells, but interpretive annotation should remain. Source dimension chain explicitly shows20,40,60,100. Do not randomize holes on every render; compose a fixed legible pattern.

Curved canopy ribs: along one balcony edge, repeat ribs at0.20spacing. Each rib consists of a vertical portion from parapet/top floor to y=1.65–1.85, a quarter-circle of radius0.65–0.9, then straight overhead run at y≈2.55–2.7. In a YZ section: start(0,0.6), vertical to(0,1.85); arc center(z=0.8,y=1.85) from angleπ toπ/2 gives z0→0.8,y1.85→2.65; continue toward z3.2 at y2.65. Prefer square-section extruded path or a segmented rectangular tube with enough segments (16–24). Source profile looks square/rectangular metal, not round pipe. Repeat10–16 ribs parallel; connect perpendicular overhead crossbars at0.20 or0.40 to make lattice. Keep the visual space below open.

Functional infill: 6–10 thin off-white canvas boards of assorted sizes leaning in larger storage openings; 3–4 vertical wire separators in one tall bay for canvas storage; a small stack of paper/boards as a drying rack; sparse thin horizontal trays; one or two plants with muted green leaves emerging through grid openings. Paintings should be abstract placeholders or cropped source painting textures only if available, not invented artwork presented as Afaf’s. A couple small framed planes can convey art without false attribution.

- “Drying rack — repeated horizontal trays”
- “Curved rib — the vertical screen turns overhead”

Do not stretch fixed steel to pretend the built structure mechanically transforms.

## 3. Résidence du Parc 5 (RDP5)

### What sources actually support

Omar p23 supplies a comprehensive plan; p22 has four portal-elevation studies and two interior views; p23 lower half an additional rendered interior. Status: concept design,2019. The prose explicitly says services consolidate to a central cruciform, while full-length screens subdivide/open the plan. The overall outline is not a rectangle: a tapering/diagonal public wing at the left connects to a large bedroom wing on the right, separated by the central stair/elevator zone. The right wing contains a circular shared lobby/hub and curved bathroom/bedroom boundaries. Replacing the whole design with a cross on a rectangle would be overly generic.

### Recommended presentation

Use a roofless, low-wall axonometric apartment model with a traced irregular floor outline, essential partitions, service/core blocks, movable screens and simple furniture volumes. Add a second close-up “Portal & screen” study at full room height, since the source elevations show unusual offset arches and rounded openings clearly. Label site-wide plan proportions as interpretive; it is not a surveyed floor plan.

### Portal & screen detailed sub-study

p22 top elevations show four different configurations/opening profiles; do not assume they are snapshots of one mechanically morphing doorway. They include an arch, asymmetric rounded upper corner, and a tall narrow round-headed opening. Use discrete source-inspired profiles with clear selection, not squishy continuously morphing architecture.

Build three consecutive portal planes along a central axis, approx1.8–2.4wide×2.7high with openings1.2–1.7wide. Portal reveal depth0.15–0.25. Outer white wall, painted inner reveal in cobalt blue / mustard yellow / muted green. In source corridor thumbnail, yellow portals are nested with blue side reveals and warm wood along lower sides. Main living render shows a green niche at foreground and blue/yellow successive portals in distance. Use Shape holes to create genuine arch cutouts. Round-head opening can use a rectangular lower portion + semicircle top. Offset arch uses straight left jamb, flat/short top then large rounded upper-right corner; narrower version has semicircular top at a smaller radius.

A timber sliding screen can be a full-height0.04thick panel next to a portal, placed on a thin ceiling/floor track and translating into pocket/along adjacent wall. Source text supports full-length screens but exact hardware is not visible. Keep animation labelled “open/closed spatial study.” Show two zones changing connection using screen position, not arbitrary rotating walls. Opposite feature wall is dark charcoal open shelving with irregular rectangular compartments, alternating white/wood cabinet fronts; low base white cabinets. Include this only in close-up, not every room.

## 4. Landship — greater detail addition to prior notes

Keep previous model-geometry-notes.md for overall frame geometry. Further source-supported parts:
- Screw piles/level supports first, then continuous prefabricated keel/floor beam, then closely spaced ribs, then furnished modules: p28 numbered4-stage assembly explicitly supports this ordering.
- Interior program sequence from p28 left to right: entrance; three seating modules; shower; WC; cooking; sink; foldable bed+library; six bed+storage modules; entrance. This is a16-module illustrated sequence; use that as an explicitly schematic programme layout. Number of structural ribs may be one greater or different at ends; avoid claiming exact engineering count.
- Seating section is near one end and uses low timber box seats with pale cushions facing glazing. Wet-service modules have opaque partitions/doors. Cooking/sink modules use compact cabinetry. Sleeping stretch combines low timber box bases, folded shelves/bed platforms and storage. Represent these with simple honest solids while keeping a continuous clear central passage.
- Source p28 notes glass panels can open up to15degrees and ramps can be closed. A panel tilt control0–15degrees is unusually well grounded. Do not swing the entire glass wall as one piece; animate selected individual bay panes around their edge. End ramp can pivot toward upright/closed, while geometry remains schematic.
- Dark external ribs are wide flat profiles; interior timber lining and blue-gray clear glazing occupy alternating side/roof faces. Openings trace changing upper profiles. Earth terrain should be subtle; cabin rests above it on slender posts with a level floor.

Useful annotations: “Screw pile — levels the cabin above terrain”; “Keel — prefabricated base”; “Rib — repeated structural profile”; “Module — program fitted between ribs”; “Glazing — opens up to15° (portfolio note)”; “Entrance ramp — can close.” Assembly should separate actual component groups rather than exploding every plank.
